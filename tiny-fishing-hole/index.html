A new discovery.

Tiny Fishing Hole is a gentle fishing and collection game. Hold to lower your hook, drift towards a fish, then release to reel it home.

The expanded Android edition is in testing. Features can differ between test builds. This page supports the six-water release candidate.

Contact supportPrivacy policy

Keep fishing at your own pace

Fishing is unlimited. Every landed catch earns Coins and XP. Your first twenty catches each day add a small bonus, refreshed at 4 a.m. on your local device clock. There are no streaks, missed-day penalties or paid fishing turns.

Open Places for the XP and discoveries needed for your next water. Longer lines in Rod reach deeper fish. Completing a collection earns a free themed rod; optional Coin finishes and personal size medals give you more goals.

Remove Ads and restore a purchase

The Android edition offers a permanent Remove Ads purchase through Google Play. The displayed price comes from Play in your local currency. Paying removes interstitial advertising; fish, locations, rewards and progression stay identical.

Open Rod → Help & privacy → Ads & purchases → Restore Purchases while connected, using the same Google Play account. Pending payments unlock Remove Ads after Play completes them. Cancelled or failed purchases grant no entitlement. Verified ownership is retained offline; successful Play refreshes reconcile refunds and revocations.

Purchases are unavailable in the desktop and prototype editions, and for unknown or under-13 age groups. Advertising may be unavailable while the app is being reviewed or if consent/connectivity requirements are unmet.

Your saved progress

Your collection, Coins, records and equipment stay on your device. There is no cloud save. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes fishing progress. Restoring a purchase restores Remove Ads, not your fishing save.

If a catch cannot save, keep the game open, free storage and choose Retry save. The fish and rewards save together before its result appears. A recovered backup may lack the most recent action.

Need a hand?

Email diamondsunadmin@gmail.com with your device model, game version and what happened. Please do not send passwords, card details or a full date of birth. For purchase support, share only the relevant Google Play order reference.
