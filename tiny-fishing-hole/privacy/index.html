Your quiet corner.

Tiny Fishing Hole keeps fishing progress on your device. The Android edition uses Google services for occasional advertising, privacy choices and the optional Remove Ads purchase.

Who we are

Tiny Fishing Hole is published by Diamond Sun / SmoothSteve. Contact diamondsunadmin@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. We do not operate game accounts, a gameplay analytics backend or cloud saves.

Information kept on your device

  • Fish discoveries, catch totals, size records, Coins, XP, equipment, cosmetics, waters and saved catch results.
  • Daily bonus counts, local reset-day markers, advertising frequency limits and sound settings.
  • A broad age band: unknown, under 13, 13–17 or 18 and over. We do not request or store a date of birth.
  • Google consent choices managed by its User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • A signed Google Play purchase receipt, separate from fishing progress, to verify Remove Ads offline.

Fishing saves are not uploaded to us. Clearing app data or uninstalling deletes local progress. The game does not use Android app-data backup. A Play purchase can be restored separately on the same Play account.

Advertising and information Google processes

The Android edition includes Google Mobile Ads and UMP. Eligible players may receive an interstitial after acknowledging a saved repeat catch. Ads require both five active minutes and ten catches between displays, with at most three per local day. Discoveries, records and collection celebrations are excluded.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share these categories for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention:

  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address.
  • App activity and interactions, including ad views and taps.
  • App information and performance diagnostics, including crashes and hangs.
  • Device or other identifiers, including app-set identifiers and advertising ID where permitted.

Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy for its processing, retention and protections. The game does not request precise GPS location, contacts, photos or microphone access.

Age and UMP consent gates apply before ad requests. Where required, privacy choices are under Rod → Help & privacy → Ads & purchases → Privacy options. Fishing works when advertising, consent services or connectivity are unavailable. Remove Ads owners receive no interstitials or further ad requests.

Age groups

The game is intended for teens and adults aged 13+. Unknown and under-13 sessions remain ad-free, with purchasing unavailable. Teen sessions use age-restricted treatment, an under-age-of-consent setting and conservative general-audience ad content. We store only the selected age band locally and do not ask children for contact information or purchases.

Google Play purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Play processes payments, maintains purchase history and provides purchase status and signed receipts to the game. We do not receive card or bank details through the app. Google’s payment services operate under their own privacy terms.

The game verifies and acknowledges completed purchases. Pending, cancelled or failed payments do not grant ownership. Verified ownership stays available offline; successful Play refreshes reconcile refunds or revocations. Reinstalling requires Restore Purchases using the same Play account. This does not restore fishing progress.

Verification occurs in the app without our own server. Offline receipt caching cannot detect a refund until Play successfully refreshes ownership. We do not add an account or remote player profile.

Support, retention and choices

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you include. We use it to answer the request and keep it only as needed for support and legitimate recordkeeping. Do not send passwords, full payment details or a date of birth. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence, subject to records we must retain.

You can delete local game information through Android’s app storage settings or by uninstalling, which permanently removes fishing progress. Manage Google-held advertising, account and payment information through Google’s privacy controls. Deleting local game data does not itself delete Google’s records.

This website and changes

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages with no added analytics scripts or tracking cookies. The host may process connection information, such as IP addresses, for security and operation. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

We update this page when the game’s data practices change. The effective date is above. Earlier offline prototype builds may contain fewer services than this expanded Android edition.
